About MAXMOND°

Ugandan DJ, mixing specialist and certified party psychiatrist...

Kampala · Uganda

Reading crowds.

MAXMOND° is a Ugandan DJ and mixing specialist from ASTRO TEENZ, based in Kampala. With over 8 years in the industry, he has mastered multiple genres and become one of East Africa's most sought-after selectors.

Known as the Party Psychiatrist, his gift is diagnosing exactly what a room needs — then prescribing the perfect blend of Amapiano, Afrobeats, Dancehall, EDM, Hip Hop, House and R&B.
